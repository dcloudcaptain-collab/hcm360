Listening for Biometric Devices

Face Recognition

Kiosk or mobile camera identifies employee by face

🤳

Face Recognition Kiosk

The face recognition kiosk captures the employee photo, identifies them via the recognition API, and pushes the time log automatically.

POST /attendance/api/timelog 
{
  "employee_id": 123,
  "log_type": "IN",
  "log_method": "FACE",
  "device_id": "KIOSK-MAIN",
  "photo_path": "/captures/2026-03-31/emp123.jpg"
}
Kiosk Status
Device IDLocationStatus
Configure kiosks in Settings → Face Recognition

RFID Card Reader

Employee taps their RFID card on the reader device

🏷

RFID Tap-to-Log

RFID readers detect the employee's card tag and push time logs to the API. Each card's tag is mapped to an employee record (via employee_no or UUID).

POST /attendance/api/timelog 
{
  "rfid_tag": "A1B2C3D4E5",
  "log_type": "IN",
  "log_method": "RFID",
  "device_id": "RFID-GATE-01",
  "location": "Main Entrance"
}
Registered Readers
Device IDLocationStatus
Configure readers in Settings → RFID
